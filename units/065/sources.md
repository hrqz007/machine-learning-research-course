# 一级资料与核验

2026-10-07实际读取下列一级来源。Boyd书第5章关键核对为Slater/KKT，非对教材文字的复制。

1. https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf
   用途：Chapter5: weak/strong duality, Slater condition and KKT; original algebra/examples reconstructed for this lesson。

2. https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/~cjlin/papers/libsvm.pdf
   用途：C-SVC formulation and implementation background。

3. https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/svm.html
   用途：precomputed Gram semantics and signed coefficients。

4. https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/generated/sklearn.svm.SVC.html
   用途：poly kernel, gamma, coef0 and fit/predict shapes。

5. https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs4780/2018fa/lectures/lecturenote14.html
   用途：kernel PSD properties and closure background。

6. https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.17.0/reference/optimize.minimize-slsqp.html
   用途：numerical constrained minimizer and success/tolerance inspection。

手算、完整推导结构、实验代码、合成数据与图为课程原创组织。数值KKT审计不等于形式化证明。
